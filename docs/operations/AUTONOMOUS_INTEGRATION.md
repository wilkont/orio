# Autonomous wallet, gas and ledger integration

November 20, 2026. Qualification source and disconnected execution evidence; no hosted migration or deployment is performed by this document.

## Customer authorization and execution

`contracts/sdk/autonomous-wallet.mjs` builds purchase approvals/calls, EIP-712 withdrawal consent, admission, whole-epoch settlement, fixed-recipient payout claims, cancellation/expiry release and zero-protocol-fee transfers. All amounts/nonces/times use canonical integer strings. Chain, contract, epoch, recipient, amount and minimum payout are bound to customer consent. Builders reject mainnet and do not sign, send or choose credentials. They accept trusted deployment/snapshot inputs; deployment admission and live freshness checks belong to the caller.

Redemption has three separately executable phases: admission, settlement and payout delivery. The wallet must distinguish tokens locked, payout funded and payout delivered. Each phase needs its own complete network-cost authorization; a quote for admission is not authorization to charge for later settlement/claim. A customer may receive several claims at the same recipient; the claim contract delivers their aggregate. The module is an application connection layer, not a completed hosted consumer authentication/passkey interface.

The actual EntryPoint/account/paymaster tests execute purchase, ERC-1271 withdrawal admission, settlement, claim and transfer while the account has zero ETH. The database integration reserves prepaid user gas before protected permit signing, checks actual EntryPoint debit, separately charges user gas, refunds unused estimates and prevents duplicate closure. Reserve/claim cash and gas balances use distinct accounts/assets. Local synthetic funding, KMS protocol emulation, block anchors and zero L1 charges are explicitly not live AWS, Base finality, gas-conversion pricing or L1/bundler allocation evidence.

The company paymaster can withhold its own service. A separate test disables it and relays a smart-account owner's signed withdrawal through an unrelated native-gas caller; the controller accepts that request without company approval. This does not fund an alternative relayer or complete its user-gas billing. Current qualification accounts pin their paymaster for account execution; public admission/settlement/claim are the protocol fallback. Do not describe the gas service as unconditionally available.

## Finalized accounting

The controller emits explicit start/checkpoint boundaries for purchases, paid or empty epochs, zero-supply reconciliation and company distributions. Withdrawal allocation events identify owner and recipient. `finalized-autonomous.ts` requires two configured readers, canonical finalized receipts, exact event encoding and corresponding token mint/burn/custody movements. Claim funding must match both aggregate vault-to-claims cash and each recipient credit; actual payment requires a separate claims-to-recipient transfer and claim event. Extra or substituted movements fail verification.

`AutonomousSettlement` independently recomputes purchase/redemption economics with the approved monetary engine, including rounding and zero-supply reopening. Checkpoints must exactly match supply, price, reserve, company fees and funded pending claims. Database row locks serialize imports. All event ownership, accounts, journals, immutable facts, state/version and chain-log cursor updates commit together. Identical retries return the original receipt; conflicting/reused/out-of-order evidence fails. Mint/burn events cannot enter as ordinary wallet transfers. Errors leave prior state intact. No customer records or credentials enter reports.

Funded claims are segregated cash/liabilities, not current reserve backing. Zero-supply reconciliation may reopen with prior funded claims outstanding because their cash has already left backing and remains entirely owed to recipients. Unfunded obligations cannot be passed off as funded claims. Rounding remains in separate USD54 memo accounts. Company distributions debit only recorded company cash. Missing claim deliveries, wallet transfers or company distributions stop reconciliation instead of concealing the difference.

`pending-migrations/022_autonomous_settlement.sql` creates owner-only tables with append-only facts; it grants no runtime or public permissions. Existing SQL and monetary importers remain unchanged. The new migration is tested in isolated temporary PostgreSQL databases and is not applied to hosted customer data. A hosted clone rehearsal, checked original-database change and a restricted importer service remain required.

## Migration and deployment boundary

These changed candidate artifacts need a new pinned deployment manifest; the previous candidate was not deployed. The existing deployed controller cannot be silently redirected into this accounting ABI. Preserve both histories. Existing customer assets/liabilities require a separate verified migration or customer-authorized redemption/reissue plan; no funds sweep or reference-price reset is authorized. For an empty original genesis, verify both providers' pinned final state, zero supply/reserves/rounding/company fees/pending obligations, retained original $1 price, no prior monetary cycle and disabled old execution before any client routing change. A non-genesis or nonempty source must be rejected by such an empty-state plan.

Live two-source feed admission must include proxy powers, update cadence, source independence, sequencer-outage/recovery treatment and funding for permissionless updates. Chainlink's [EVM integration guidance](https://docs.chain.link/docs/consuming-data-feeds) requires L2 sequencer checks; its [sequencer guide](https://docs.chain.link/data-feeds/l2-sequencer-feeds) describes outage/recovery handling. Pyth documents that [Core prices are stored on chain and its contracts are upgradeable](https://docs.pyth.network/pro/contract-addresses), so immutable consumer addresses alone do not remove provider control. No current feed admission or complete adapter is asserted here.

Remaining release work includes live feed adapters and their evidence, larger-epoch processing, hosted wallet/recovery/provider connections, alternative relay/gas funding and actual Base cost allocation, deployment/signing permission scoped to new artifacts, hosted SQL/importer qualification, source/target migration evidence, actual reserve/customer rights and capital arrangements, and owner release acceptance. External review remains advisory. Production financial execution remains disabled.
