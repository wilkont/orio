# Autonomous redemption qualification candidate

November 18, 2026. Source candidate only; existing deployments are unchanged and financial operations remain disabled.

## Authority and behavior

`ORIOAutonomousController` is a separate version, restricted to Base Sepolia and local chain 31337. It creates its own currency, lock adapter, reserve vault and funded withdrawal claims. It has no operator, company valuation signer, owner, governance configuration, upgrade, discretionary pause or reserve-sweep entry point. The existing controller and deployment compiler remain unchanged. This is not an in-place upgrade or an authorized migration.

Customers authorize EIP-712 requests binding chain, controller, epoch, request ID, owner, recipient, amount, minimum payout, nonce and expiry. Anyone can relay these authorizations. The first admission freezes the ORIO reference price and supply; purchases cannot change the snapshot while the epoch is open. Immutable wall-clock admission periods admit at most 64 requests. The local qualification uses 60 seconds; this is not an approved production parameter. Requests must produce the signed positive minimum payout at admission. Cancelling the final live entry clears the epoch immediately; other cancelled entries consume capacity until clearing. Active valid redemptions still freeze purchases for their snapshot period, so sustained redemption demand can delay issuance. A full queue requires waiting for the next epoch, not discretionary operator selection. Network ordering and queue spam remain availability risks.

Every request in an epoch has the same immutable close-plus-120-second expiry; callers cannot select per-customer expiry windows. This is a qualification parameter, not an approved production parameter. After closing, anyone can settle the entire admitted queue. Callers cannot choose a subset. The common snapshot price, current lower-source master valuation and deterministic authorization rules select eligible requests. Expired, cancelled, below-minimum and nonpaying dust requests are unlocked without burning. Owners can cancel before closing; anyone can release expired locks. An entirely expired/cancelled epoch clears without a price feed. Two independent immutable on-chain feed addresses replace company valuation signatures: positive completed rounds, observations no older than 300 seconds, no future observations and at most 2% disagreement relative to the lower price. Issuance/reopening additionally require the approved $0.995–$1.005 band. No dollar-peg assumption bypasses backing checks.

Settlement preserves the 1% conversion fee, exact rounding, ordinary company floor-20%/holder remainder, redemption exclusion and full-redemption exception. It funds all accepted payouts in a separate immutable claim contract before recording claims. Anyone can deliver a claim only to its authorized recipient. A recipient-specific token restriction cannot veto other recipients' claims; failed transfers preserve the funded claim. Claims accumulate per recipient. Company fees can be delivered publicly only to the immutable company recipient, within the accrued company-fee account. Reserve assets cannot be swept through that method.

Zero-supply reconciliation is public and checks reserve accounting and pending-claim funding. Reopening preserves the last reference price and applies the zero-holder company-fee exception. No genesis/residual escrow or discretionary concentration threshold is introduced.

## Failure containment and truthful claims

Stale/disagreeing/invalid feeds or impaired reserves reject settlement atomically. Restored valid data and backing permit anyone to retry before expiry. Expiry always permits lock release without a company key or fresh feed; this restores tokens, not a guaranteed stablecoin payout. Stablecoin-wide freezes, censorship of the vault/claim contract, unavailable networks and insufficient backing can still prevent payouts. The source therefore supports the narrower statement: **this candidate has no ORIO company approval key on its redemption path**. It does not establish that no third party can affect withdrawals or that payouts are always available.

The feeds are constructor dependencies, not admitted live providers. Code-hash pins reject replacement bytecode but do not detect changes behind an upgradeable proxy or establish independent economic sources. Feed administrators can affect availability/data; stablecoin issuers retain their own asset controls. Production source admission must identify these retained powers and verify deployment addresses, proxy implementations, update behavior and independence. No such live admission is asserted here.

## Verification and integration boundary

Disconnected VM tests compare actual reserve, supply, price and rounding transitions against the separate exact monetary engine. Tests cover public relays/settlement/claims, authority rejection, replay and domain binding, expiry, stale/invalid/disagreeing prices, payout isolation, full redemption/reopening, a one-atom residual and the full 64-request capacity with distinct owners and recipients, invalid-admission progress and uniform expiry. Compilation enforces contract and initialization size limits. Fixture feeds/assets and published synthetic VM keys are disconnected test tools, never live reserve infrastructure.

Before deployment or production qualification, complete: admitted real feed adapters; fixed parameter acceptance; multi-epoch capacity/fairness, large-epoch multi-chunk progress and adversarial economic assessment; the controller-event/control-ledger checkpoint adapter; wallet admission/claim/retry UX; user-paid gas quotation, authorization, public-relayer execution and reconciliation; monitored keeper redundancy; migration and recovery qualification; actual reserve/customer-rights arrangements and release acceptance. Native transaction gas is paid by each caller in these tests; this is not completed end-user gas abstraction. No gas charge is added to conversion fees or reserve backing.

This candidate intentionally has a fixed master and no callable reserve migration. Autonomous master replacement required by the broader platform is not implemented here; a replacement design must preserve withdrawal rights without reintroducing unilateral discretionary control. The existing vault's controller-only migration function has no reachable forwarding method in this controller. Do not point existing wallets or accounting services at this ABI without the verified adapter and migration plan. No production key is used, no contract is deployed and no financial activation follows from local tests. External review remains advisory.
