# Wallet recovery qualification candidate

The separate ORIORecoverableGasAccount and factory preserve the original qualification wallet's code path. They accept only Base Sepolia/local qualification chains and reject Base mainnet. No public wallet or real assets have been deployed. External review remains advisory; this is not production qualification or approval of operational recovery parameters.

The wallet has no ORIO employee override or recovery function that transfers assets. Its immutable enrollment includes two to five guardian addresses, a quorum of at least two, and a configurable waiting period bounded to two through thirty days. These are candidate technical bounds, not an approved identity or estate policy. Different addresses do not prove different controlling people; actual guardian selection, ownership verification and independent custody must be qualified before use.

Guardians sign an EIP-712 proposal binding account, chain, replacement owner, current owner revision, monotonic recovery nonce and authorization expiry. Signatures must come from distinct, sorted enrolled guardians. ERC-1271 signers are supported. Proposing a recovery consumes its nonce, freezes new account signatures and execution, and starts the delay. Execution batches check the freeze before every call. Already authorized external calls, pre-existing token allowances and already locked protocol settlements are not globally undone or frozen by this mechanism.

Anyone can relay a valid proposal or finalize it after the delay. The existing owner can sign a proposal-specific cancellation for a relayer; cancelling cannot replay an earlier proposal's approvals. Owner revision changes on finalization. Both gas-permit consent and account transaction signatures wrap their underlying digest with that revision, so returning to a former owner does not revive its old authorizations. The offline signing SDK produces exact contract-compatible typed data using canonical integer strings.

While recovery is pending, ERC-4337 account execution is frozen, including ordinary cancellation calls through the wallet. Cancellation therefore needs the separately authorized relayer workflow, with a quoted user gas charge collected through the gas controls. That live cancellation relayer/collection adapter is not implemented here. The finalization/proposal relayer gas and responsibility policy must also be completed; local tests do not imply free sponsored recovery. The recovered owner does execute successfully through the actual reference EntryPoint/paymaster with zero account ETH in the local test.

Current-owner veto is suitable for contesting mistaken lost-key recovery, but an attacker controlling that same key can also veto. This candidate does not solve every compromised-account scenario. Guardian replacement requires a separately qualified migration/enrollment design; guardians are immutable in this candidate. Device/passkey enrollment, passkey-specific verification, notifications, identity and deceased-holder procedures, abuse handling, authorization UX and emergency compromise response remain open. No claim of completed consumer recovery is made.

Nine actual EVM tests cover deterministic factory binding, repeated deployment after recovery, quorum and duplicate rejection, expired/wrong approvals, delayed execution, freeze, cancellation/replay, owner-revision rollback protection, standard/offline SDK hash agreement recovered-owner execution through the reference EntryPoint/paymaster, mid-batch recovery freeze/rollback and explicit mainnet-deployment rejection. Keys and funding are published synthetic fixtures only.
