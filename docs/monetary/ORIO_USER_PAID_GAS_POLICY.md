# User-paid gas policy — confirmed requirement

Every purchase, redemption, ORIO transfer and other user-authorized on-chain action pays its applicable Base network fee. ORIO Company fronts native ETH through its Gas Treasury and collects the corresponding authorized user charge. Users need no ETH balance, gas wallet, network configuration or manual fee selection. This is operational abstraction, not a general company gas subsidy.

The 1% conversion fee applies only to purchase/redemption monetary value. Ordinary transfer protocol fee is zero; gas remains payable. Gas never generates conversion-fee revenue or appreciation, changes the monetary redemption price, mints ORIO or draws customer backing.

Purchase display: purchase amount, conversion fee deducted from that amount, separate maximum network charge, total authorized debit and expected ORIO quantity. Redemption display: gross value, conversion fee, network charge, final net proceeds and explicit proceeds-deduction consent if supported. Transfer display: recipient's exact amount, zero protocol fee, separate gas denomination/maximum and total debit. A gas payment in ORIO is a distinct treasury payment, not a conversion or redemption.

Bind authorization to owner, action/call digests, chain, all operations, denomination, exchange-rate evidence, expiry and maximum charge. Never exceed the cap without renewed consent. Credit/refund unused estimates. Quote an entire multi-operation action, including creation/approval/validation/settlement overhead where applicable. Do not present a guaranteed network price before finality.

Stablecoin additional balance, ORIO payment, prepaid balance and authorized redemption proceeds are candidate collection mechanisms. The current durable controller accepts only independently funded prepaid balances. Other quote methods are represented but cannot reserve/execute until their atomic collection and recovery adapters exist.

All failure causes currently require review, including user failure, platform failure, network failure, revert and unknown execution. No unapproved platform-failure debit is finalized. The user's hold remains a pending obligation during investigation; this is not indefinite-forfeiture authority. Approve case handling, deadlines, communications, refunds and company loss allocation before release.
