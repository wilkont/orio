# ORIO SPC 3 context

Ledger A is the internal financial control ledger. It records journal entries and application entitlements. Ledger B is finalized on-chain ownership and execution. Neither a database edit nor a local test creates blockchain ownership.

A journal is an immutable, balanced set of debits and credits in explicitly named asset units. Account normal balance determines whether debits or credits increase the account. A settlement reference identifies one external economic event. Retrying the same event must not create a second journal.

An outbox event commits in the same transaction as its journal. Delivery is at least once; recipients must deduplicate. A lease token fences stale worker acknowledgements. An external checkpoint is a sequence/hash retained independently of the database administrator.

Financial activation requires release-specific signed accountable acceptance for legal, security, custody, reserve/financial and operational readiness. Per the November 13 owner instruction, external independent review is advisory and one authorized owner may cover multiple roles. Tests do not establish legal status, actual custody or reserve solvency. See docs/adr/0002-owner-accountable-release-policy.md; it supersedes prior independent-review requirements.

Confirmed ordinary conversions charge 1%, allocating 20% to company revenue and 80% to eligible holders. Purchases appreciate existing holdings before minting. Redemptions exclude burned tokens from appreciation. Full redemption allocates the entire fee to the company. Bootstrap, fixed-point rounding, and residual policies remain unresolved.

The November 13 revised supplemental directive confirms user-paid network gas on every purchase, redemption, ordinary ORIO transfer and other user-authorized on-chain operation. Ordinary transfers still have zero protocol fee. Company Gas Treasury fronts ETH operationally; users need no ETH. Gas charges, ETH expenditure and refunds are separate from monetary backing, conversion revenue, appreciation and token issuance. Bind all operations, complete network costs, denomination/rate, expiry and maximum charge to user authorization; credit unused charges and prevent duplicate billing. No general company gas subsidy is authorized.

The final monetary rules were explicitly approved: "Approve economics; external review stays advisory". Original genesis starts at $1; zero-holder purchases and full-supply redemption allocate their entire collected fee to company. No genesis or residual-supply escrow exists. Company ordinary allocation floors its entitlement and holders receive the rest of the rounded collected fee. Every positive remaining supply receives that allocation. Mint/payout floor, conversion fees ceil in settlement units, and all rounding residuals stay explicit and segregated. Reopening retains the last valid reference after reconciliation, never automatically resets to $1. See ADR 0003 and docs/monetary/ORIO_MONETARY_POLICY_PROPOSAL.md. Live-money execution remains disabled pending actual implementation, technical/operational readiness and accountable release acceptance, not another economics confirmation. This approval does not alter AWS spending/access scope.

The qualification-only ERC-4337 account/factory/paymaster accept only 84532/31337 and reject Base mainnet. They verify independent account and reservation-operator gas consent and execute through the pinned reference EntryPoint in local VM tests. The server must commit a prepared reservation before obtaining a treasury signature, then bind its final UserOperation hash before broadcast; it cannot cancel a possibly signed execution. Migration 009 makes that binding immutable. A local ledger/VM bridge is not proof of public-network finality, bundler rules, Base fee attribution or production readiness. See docs/qualification/PAYMASTER_QUALIFICATION.md.

Local migrations 010/011 add gas-only prepayment deposit accounting and withdrawal holds/payout verification. Customer funds, company ETH and monetary reserves remain separate. A withdrawal must preserve its funded gas action; it can cancel only together with unused gas/ETH holds before preparation. Exact payout attribution is bound to its own successful UserOperation log range, not merely the containing bundle. Source/receipt records are append-only; external review is advisory and live-money execution is still disabled.
